Flow Granada is a residential community in Riyadh offering fully furnished 2 and 3-bedroom homes designed for comfort, sustainability, and modern living. These homes feature custom-made furniture, earth-based materials, and thoughtful design intended to foster well-being and creativity. The community is located within the Granada district of Riyadh. Flow Granada is part of a larger development within the Granada district, which also includes Granada Mall, a large shopping center. The mall is located on the eastern ring road between exits 8 and 9 in the Ghernata District.
