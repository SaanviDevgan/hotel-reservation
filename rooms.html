Types Of Rooms

Standard Room (Single/Double) Single Room: One single bed for one person. Double Room: One double bed for two people. Twin Room: Two separate single beds for two people.
Deluxe Room Larger and more comfortable than a standard room. May include better views, seating areas, or upgraded furnishings.
Superior Room A step above deluxe, often with more space or a premium location (e.g. higher floor, better view). May include extra amenities.
Suite Includes one or more bedrooms and a separate living area. Types: Junior Suite: A larger room with a partial living area. Executive Suite: Separate living and sleeping areas. Presidential or Royal Suite: Luxurious and spacious with premium amenities; often includes multiple rooms.
Accessible Room Designed for guests with disabilities. Features may include wider doorways, grab bars, lower sinks, and roll-in showers.
Family Room Larger rooms or suites designed for families. May include multiple beds, bunk beds, or sofa beds.
Cabana Room A room next to a pool or beach, sometimes with private outdoor space.
